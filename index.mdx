# Build, launch, and grow your token with WPizza

WPizza is an all-in-one platform for creating, launching, trading, managing, and securing tokens on **BNB Smart Chain**.

From token creation and presales to Pizza Curve trading, PancakeSwap liquidity, and token locking — WPizza gives you the tools to manage the complete token lifecycle.
