# What is WPizza?

**WPizza is an all-in-one token platform built on BNB Smart Chain (BSC), designed to make token creation, launching, trading, liquidity management, and token security simpler and more accessible.**

WPizza was created to give individuals, developers, and project teams the tools they need to take an idea from a token concept to a fully managed on-chain project.

From creating a token and launching it on the **Pizza Curve** to managing liquidity, running presales, and locking assets, WPizza brings the essential parts of the token lifecycle together in one ecosystem.

## Built by OpenNode Tech

WPizza is a product developed and maintained by **OpenNode Tech**, a registered technology company focused on application development and maintenance.

OpenNode Tech builds software and infrastructure designed to make emerging technologies more accessible, practical, and easier to use.

WPizza is one of the products developed as part of this vision, with a focus on providing accessible tools for the BNB Smart Chain ecosystem.

## Built to be accessible

WPizza is designed to be **free to use**.

The goal is to remove unnecessary barriers for people who want to create and manage tokens without requiring complex technical infrastructure or expensive software.

Whether you are experimenting with your first token, building a community project, or developing a larger blockchain product, WPizza is designed to provide the tools needed to move from an idea to an on-chain project.

## Your privacy matters

WPizza is designed with privacy in mind.

**WPizza does not collect personal data from users.**

Blockchain interactions naturally take place on public networks, meaning transactions and wallet addresses can be visible on-chain. However, WPizza does not require users to provide personal information simply to use the platform.

The platform is designed to interact with your wallet and the blockchain rather than building a centralized profile of your personal information.

> **Your wallet is your identity on-chain.**

## Built for the BNB Smart Chain ecosystem

WPizza is focused on **BNB Smart Chain**, providing tools that help users manage different stages of a token's lifecycle.

The ecosystem includes:

- **Token Creation** — Create taxed or tax-free tokens.
- **WPizzaDex** — Launch tokens on the Pizza Curve and enable immediate trading.
- **Presales** — Create and manage token presales.
- **Liquidity** — Add and manage liquidity on PancakeSwap V2.
- **WPizza Lock** — Lock tokens and liquidity and view locked assets.
- **Token Management** — Manage tokens and their associated on-chain functionality.

Together, these tools are designed to help projects move from **creation to launch, trading, liquidity, and long-term management**.

## Transparency by design

Transparency is one of the fundamental principles behind WPizza.

The smart contracts powering the protocol are **verified on-chain**, allowing users and developers to inspect the contract code and understand how the protocol works.

There are no intentionally hidden contract implementations or undisclosed contract logic behind the core WPizza protocol.

Users should always be able to verify what they are interacting with and independently inspect the contracts used by the platform.

### Why verification matters

Smart contracts control important on-chain operations such as token creation, swaps, liquidity, and asset locking.

By making contract code publicly verifiable, WPizza makes it possible for users and developers to:

- Inspect the contract implementation.
- Understand how protocol functions work.
- Verify contract addresses.
- Review permissions and ownership.
- Independently analyze the protocol's on-chain behavior.

Transparency does not eliminate all risks associated with blockchain applications, but it provides users with the information needed to make more informed decisions.

## Our goal

WPizza was created with a simple goal:

> **Give people accessible tools to create, launch, and manage blockchain projects while keeping the infrastructure transparent and easy to understand.**

We believe that building on-chain should not require every project to build its own infrastructure from scratch.

WPizza provides the foundation, while users and project teams decide what they want to build.

## The WPizza vision

WPizza aims to become a complete ecosystem for token projects on BNB Smart Chain.

The journey starts with a token, but it does not have to end there.

A project can be created, launched through the Pizza Curve, traded by its community, reach its required liquidity threshold, migrate toward open-market liquidity, and continue to be managed through the broader WPizza ecosystem.

This creates a complete lifecycle:

**Create → Launch → Trade → Grow → Graduate → Manage**

WPizza is built to support that journey.
